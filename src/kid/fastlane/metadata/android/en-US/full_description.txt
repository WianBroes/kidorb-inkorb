KidOrb is a drawing app made for children: a ball rolls across the screen and leaves a trail of colour behind it, like a ball dipped in paint. Simple, with no complicated text, no levels to unlock and no pressure.

What you can do:
• Roll the ball with a fingertip and watch the colour trail appear
• Pick a colour from the rainbow and draw at your own pace
• Brush, eraser, and several balls to unlock depending on your mood
• Locked zoom to stay focused on the drawing, not on the settings
• Export the finished drawing to keep it or print it

No ads. No account to create. No internet connection needed: KidOrb works entirely offline and collects no data.

Made by a parent to keep children busy with a simple, satisfying gesture: roll a ball and watch the colours appear.
